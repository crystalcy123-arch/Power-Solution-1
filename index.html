Why Class A customers use storage

Under the Industrial Conservation Initiative, a Class A customer pays Global Adjustment based on its share of demand during the province's highest-demand hours. A battery that discharges during those hours lowers that share.

Lower peak demand can also reduce the demand charges on your delivery bill. The assessment shows how much of each applies to your site.

Questions we hear first

Does the assessment cost anything?

No. The assessment is free and carries no obligation. We ask only for your data authorization and a 30-minute meeting to review the results.

What if Ontario changes the Class A rules?

We track IESO and Ontario Energy Board changes. The agreement is written so that pricing is reset if the rules that drive the savings change.

What happens if Power Solution stops operating?

The system stays on your site, and the agreement gives you step-in rights so the battery keeps running.

Do you need access to my roof or to interrupt production?

No. The battery is ground-mounted outdoors and connects at your switchgear. Installation is planned around your operations.
